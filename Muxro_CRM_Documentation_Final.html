1. Introduction

Muxro CRM Cloud is a cloud-based Lead Management and Sales CRM designed for businesses that receive enquiries from multiple sources such as:

  • Website
  • Facebook
  • Google Ads
  • WhatsApp
  • IndiaMART
  • Manual Entry
  • CSV Import

The CRM automatically manages Lead Assignment, Sales Follow-ups, WhatsApp Communication, Notifications, User Management, Campaign Tracking, and Reports.

2. First Login

Login using the credentials shared by your administrator.

During your first login:

  1. Open Users.
  2. Change the password of the Admin and Sales User.

Note: This is highly recommended for security.

3. Company Profile Setup

Navigate to Workspace → Profile.

  • Fill in your Company Name, Website, Company Email, Phone, and Address.
  • Upload your Logo.
  • Click Save.

The uploaded branding will appear throughout the CRM.

4. Dashboard

The Dashboard gives a real-time overview of sales activities.

  • Business Alerts: Examples include upcoming follow-ups, best-performing user, and top campaign.
  • KPI Cards: Total Leads, Today's Leads, Pending Follow-ups, Missed Follow-ups, Won Deals, Conversion %.
  • Daily Trend Chart: Displays daily lead growth.
  • Sales Funnel: Shows New, Contacted, Interested, Converted, and Lost leads.
  • Filters: Dashboard can be filtered by Campaign and Date Range.

5. Campaign Management

Navigate to Campaigns. Campaigns are used to organize leads effectively (e.g., Facebook Leads, Google Ads, Website, IndiaMART, Trade Fair, WhatsApp).

Create Campaign:

  1. Click New Campaign.
  2. Enter Campaign Name and Description.
  3. Next → Create Form (or Skip).
  4. Assign Users.
  5. Finish. The campaign becomes active immediately.

The Campaign Card displays Campaign Status, Number of Leads, Forms, and Assigned Users.

6. Bulk Lead Import

Open a Campaign and select Import CSV. Upload your CSV file.

Required Columns: Name, Phone, Email.

After upload, the CRM automatically creates leads, removes invalid rows, and assigns leads equally among users using Round Robin Allocation.

7. Public Enquiry Form Builder

Each campaign can have unlimited forms. Open a Campaign and click Create Form.

  • Supported Fields: Name, Mobile, Email, Company, City, Dropdown, Radio, Checkbox, Paragraph, Date, File Upload, and Custom Fields.
  • Customize: Theme, Colors, Logo, and Button Style.

WhatsApp Configuration

You can configure automated WhatsApp greeting messages directly from the form builder to engage new enquiries instantly.

8. Landing Page Builder

Each form includes a landing page builder. Available sections include Header, Banner, About Company, Services, Gallery, Contact Details, Google Maps, Enquiry Form, SEO Meta, and Additional Custom Sections.

Click Publish and the CRM generates a Public URL.

Share on Website, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Google Ads, or WhatsApp. Whenever someone submits the form, the CRM automatically creates a lead, assigns a user, sends a WhatsApp Greeting (optional), and creates a follow-up.

9. Lead Management

Every Lead contains Contact Details, Campaign, Assigned User, Status, Source, Communication History, Follow-up History, and WhatsApp History.

10. Follow-up Management

The Follow-up page is the heart of the CRM. Sales users can view leads, call customers, send WhatsApp messages, send emails, update statuses, schedule next follow-ups, and view history.

  • Smart Filters: All, Upcoming, Today, Pending, Missed.
  • Additional Filters: Campaign, Source, User, Status.
  • Actions: Every lead supports WhatsApp, Email, View Details, and History.

Every follow-up automatically stores User, Date, Status, Notes, WhatsApp Messages, and Previous Follow-ups.

11. WhatsApp CRM

The CRM contains a complete WhatsApp Inbox. Features include Conversation List, Customer Search, Live Chat, and Media Preview (Images, Documents, Audio, Videos).

Shared Conversation Architecture

Each phone number owns one conversation. One Customer → One Conversation → Multiple Leads → Multiple Campaigns. No duplicate chats. No duplicate messages.

  • Incoming Messages: Whenever a customer sends a WhatsApp message, the CRM finds the conversation (creates if not available), updates chat, links all leads, and sends a notification.
  • Outgoing Messages: Users can send Text, Images, Files, and Templates. Messages are saved only once.
  • Unknown WhatsApp Number: If a customer messages without an existing lead, the CRM automatically creates a lead, assigns a campaign, assigns a user, creates a conversation, and sends a notification.

12. DND Management

The DND (Do Not Disturb) page stores customers who should not receive marketing communication.

Functions include Search, View Lead, Remove DND, and Export CSV.

13. User Management

Administrators can Add Users, Edit Users, Reset Password, Activate, Deactivate, and Delete users.

The Dashboard displays Total Users, Active, Inactive, and Admin Count.

14. Workspace

The Workspace contains essential structural settings:

  • Profile: Company Branding.
  • API & Webhooks: Configure IndiaMART, Process Sutra.
  • Communication: Configure WhatsApp API, Greeting Messages, Default Campaign, Webhook.

15. IndiaMART & Integrations

Navigate to Workspace → API → Integrations.

Configure the Pull API Key and Target Campaign for services like IndiaMART. Options include Test Connection, Fetch Now, and Auto Fetch. Every new enquiry is imported automatically.

16. Process Sutra Integration

Configure API Key and System Name. Click Test Connection. The CRM connects directly with Process Sutra workflow automation.

17. WhatsApp Business API Integration

Supported officially via Gupshup.

  • Configure API Key, Source Number, App Name, App ID, and Test Number.
  • Webhook URL is generated automatically.
  • Click Test Connection.

WhatsApp Auto Lead Creation: The Administrator selects a Default Campaign. Whenever an unknown customer messages, the CRM automatically creates a Lead, creates a Conversation, assigns a User, and starts the follow-up.

18. Notifications

CRM automatically reminds users. Notifications include Upcoming Follow-up, New WhatsApp, New Lead, Browser Notification, and Sound Alerts.

Upcoming reminders appear 10 minutes before follow-up.

19. Lead Assignment

Supports Round Robin Allocation. Every imported or generated lead is automatically assigned equally among available users.

20. Roles & Permissions

Role Permissions
Administrator Can access Dashboard, Campaigns, Forms, Users, Reports, Integrations, DND, WhatsApp, Settings.
Sales User Can access Assigned Leads, Follow-ups, WhatsApp, DND, Performance.
Cannot manage Users, Workspace, Integrations.

21. Best Practices

  • Create separate campaigns for each marketing source.
  • Import only clean CSV data with valid phone numbers.
  • Update lead status after every customer interaction.
  • Schedule the next follow-up before closing the current one.
  • Configure WhatsApp Business API before enabling automated messaging.
  • Use the Landing Page Builder for campaign-specific enquiry forms.
  • Review the Dashboard daily to monitor pending and missed follow-ups.
  • Keep company branding and profile information up to date.
  • Regularly monitor DND records to comply with customer communication preferences.

22. Troubleshooting

Problem Solution
Unable to login Verify username/password or contact the administrator.
CSV import failed Check required columns (Name, Phone, Email) and CSV format.
Leads not assigned Ensure at least one active user is assigned to the campaign.
WhatsApp messages not sending Verify Gupshup credentials and test the connection.
IndiaMART sync not working Check the Pull API Key and Auto-Fetch configuration.
Notifications not appearing Enable browser notifications and keep the CRM tab open.
Form not receiving enquiries Confirm the form is published and the public URL is correct.

23. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q. How many campaigns can I create?
Unlimited.

Q. How many users are supported?
Unlimited (subject to your deployment and licensing).

Q. Can one customer belong to multiple campaigns?
Yes. Multiple leads can reference the same customer while sharing a single WhatsApp conversation.

Q. Does the CRM support automatic lead assignment?
Yes. Round Robin allocation distributes leads equally among assigned users.

Q. Can I integrate IndiaMART?
Yes. Configure the Pull API Key in the Workspace.

Q. Does the CRM support WhatsApp Business API?
Yes. Official integration is available through Gupshup.

Q. Will the CRM remind users about follow-ups?
Yes. Smart reminders appear approximately 10 minutes before scheduled follow-ups.

Q. Can I create public enquiry forms and landing pages?
Yes. Each campaign can have customizable forms and landing pages with branding, SEO, and contact sections.
